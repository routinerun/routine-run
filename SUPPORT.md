# Getting help

- **How something works:** [routinerun.com/help](https://routinerun.com/help), the same
  pages as [`help/`](help/README.md) here.
- **A bug, a routine you want, an app or a service to connect, the API or MCP:**
  [an issue](https://github.com/routinerun/routine-run/issues/new/choose). We read every issue within a week. We do not
  promise a fix or a date.
- **Anything with your own data in it, a payment, or a question you would rather not ask
  in public:** support@routinerun.com. Support cannot see your routines; what you describe
  is all we have to go on.
