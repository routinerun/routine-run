# Reporting a security problem

Please do not open an issue. Report it privately, through
[a security advisory](https://github.com/routinerun/routine-run/security/advisories/new) on this repository, or by email to
support@routinerun.com with "Security" in the subject.

What is in scope: the app, the relay at `api.routinerun.com` (connections, the API and
MCP), and routinerun.com. Tell us what you found and how to see it; we answer within a
week and say what we are doing about it.

If you have a connection's secret (`rr_…`) or its address in front of you while testing,
please do not use it on somebody else's phone. If you posted one of your own anywhere
public, remove that connection in the app — Settings › Connections — and make a new one:
the old address stops at once.
