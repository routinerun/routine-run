# Check-ins

A check-in is a question the app keeps asking until you answer it, such as "Ready for “Pack the car”?" every thirty minutes. Its answers are buttons: pressing one answers the question and does the thing, such as bringing the step back. Then it stops asking.

## Starting one

On a going run, press **Not now…**, then one of:

- **Check in on this step…** for a step waiting on a reply, a parcel or the oven. The step goes to the end meanwhile (**Put it at the end meanwhile**), so the run is not held up.
- **Check in on the run…** for a run put down. The run pauses meanwhile (**Pause it meanwhile**), so its timers do not run on without you.

The sheet has four parts:

- **It asks**: the question, or empty for the usual one.
- **Remind me**: on, each time it asks a card goes to the Lock Screen and the watch. Off, it only waits in In hand and on the run's page, marked **Due**.
- **How often**: **Again and again**, every so many minutes, hours or days, or **Once**, at a time. For minutes and hours, **Only between** keeps it to part of the day.
- **Answers**: up to three. A step's are **Now**, **Next** and **Later**, plus **Last** when the step is not already at the end. A run's are **Next step**, **Pause**, **Finish** and **Stop**, or **Resume** instead of Pause when the run is paused meanwhile.

Press **Check in**.

## Answering

Press an answer on the card, or in In hand. **Not yet** is always there too — a button on the card, and in In hand behind the ⋯ beside the question:

- On the card, it asks again at its next time.
- In In hand, it hides until then, and an interval starts counting again.

![A step's check-in waiting in In hand, with its answers underneath.](check-in)

Once answered, it stops asking and says what it was answered. **Ask again** starts it over.

**Decide later**, on the Lock Screen card, is a quick one: the step goes to the end and the question waits in In hand, with no card.

## Every run of a routine

On the routine's page, tap the step on the line at the top and press **Not now…** on its card, or open the step and choose **Not now…** in its menu. Then choose **Check in on it…** under "Every run of it, from now on". Each run gets its own question when it reaches that step, counted from that moment.

## Good to know

- A check-in goes when its run does, and the orange band never counts it.
- Cards count against the 64 notifications iOS holds for the app. A longer gap or **Only between** uses fewer.
- They work the same on iOS 26.1 and 27.

## When something goes wrong

- **It keeps asking.** **Not yet** is not an answer. Press one of the others.
- **No card arrives.** **Remind me** is off, or notifications are off for the app. Look in In hand.
- **Stop it without answering.** Open it and press **Stop asking**.
