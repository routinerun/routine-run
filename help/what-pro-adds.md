# What Pro adds

More routines. That is the whole difference: Pro holds as many as you like, and every feature is the same in both.

## The price

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| **Pro, monthly** | $6.99 a month |
| **Pro, yearly** | $49.99 a year, which is $4.17 a month |
| **Lifetime** | $119.99 once, in the launch window only |

Prices are in US dollars. The App Store shows yours in your own currency. Pro arrives with the launch; in the beta everything is free.

## The launch window

For the first eight weeks after the app is released, two things are on offer and then come off by the calendar:

- **The first year of Pro is $34.99** instead of $49.99. It renews at $49.99 after that.
- **Lifetime is $119.99**, paid once. When the window closes it stops being sold, and everybody who bought it keeps it for as long as the app exists.

Apple allows one introductory offer per person, so taking the discounted first year is the reason there is no free trial. The free tier is the trial, and it has no end date.

## What does not change

- No feature is Pro only. A trigger, a check-in, a step that runs a shortcut and the Apple Watch work the same on Free.
- Nothing about a run is locked. Starting, moving on and the record of it are the same on either plan.
- There is no account. Pro belongs to your Apple Account, so any iPhone signed in to it has Pro.

## Stopping

On your iPhone: **Settings**, your name, **Subscriptions**, **Routine Run**. Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds go through Apple, at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
