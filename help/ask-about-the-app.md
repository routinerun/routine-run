# Asking how the app works

**Settings → Ask about Routine Run**, the first thing on the page.

Type a question, such as "How do I start, if my mornings are a mess?" or "What does
Keep do?", and tap **Ask**. It answers from the Routine Run method first, then says which
buttons to press. The answer appears on
the page. Ask a follow-up and it remembers what you asked before. **Start over** clears
the conversation.

## Who answers

- **iOS 27:** Apple's cloud model, which has the whole method. What you ask is used to
  answer and not kept.
- **iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence:** the model on your iPhone, which has the five-minute
  version of the method. Nothing leaves it.
- **Without Apple Intelligence:** use **Ask Claude or ChatGPT instead**.

It knows how the app works, not what is in your routines. For help with one of your
routines, open it and use **Describe changes…**.

## Asking your own assistant

**Ask Claude or ChatGPT instead** sends the app's explanation to any assistant through
the share sheet, with whatever you typed as the first question. Then carry on there.
