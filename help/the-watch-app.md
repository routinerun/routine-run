# The Watch app

The watch shows the runs your iPhone is holding and moves them on from your wrist. The iPhone does the work: the watch asks it, and says so when it is out of reach. It needs watchOS 26 or later.

## Getting it going

Open Routine Run on the iPhone once. Until then the watch says **Waiting for iPhone**.

## Raise your wrist

The watch opens on the run that is going. On it:

- **The step you are on**, its timer or how far through you are, and a row of beads.
- **Next** (the forward arrow), and **Again** where going round the step again means something.
- **The ellipsis**, for everything else.
- **The step's own buttons** underneath, such as a shortcut to run. One that has to leave the app says **Finishes on the phone**, and it runs there.

![A run on the watch: the step, its timer, the beads and Next.](watch-step)

With several runs going, swipe sideways between them. **Double Tap** presses Next, or the step's first button when the step is waiting on one.

Behind the ellipsis:

- **This step:** **Do it later** and **Do it last**. See [Pause, Later and Last](pause-later-last.md).
- **This run:** **Pause** or **Resume**, **Pin** or **Unpin**, **Come back to it** (in an hour, this evening, tomorrow, next week), and **Note**, which you can dictate.
- **Stop**, on its own, at the foot.

## The lists

Swipe back to **Running**. Swipe a run right for **Next**, left for **Again** and **Pause**. Runs that are not going wait under it, behind **Paused** and **Later**.

The button at the top right switches to **Routines**. Swipe a routine right to **Start** it, or open it for **Start**, **Turn it off** and its steps.

If a Focus is holding routines back on the iPhone, a line at the top says so, and you can show everything.

## On the watch face

- **Current Step** and **Coming up**, as complications.
- **Upcoming Routine**, in the Smart Stack when a routine is due.
- The run's Lock Screen card appears in the Smart Stack too, with Next on it.

## Siri and the Action button

Say "Next step in Routine Run", "Start Morning in Routine Run", "Routine Run current step", or "Note this in Routine Run". On an Apple Watch Ultra, the Action button can run **Do the One Thing**. See [Action button](action-button.md).

## When it goes wrong

**Goes when iPhone is back.** The iPhone is out of reach. What you pressed is kept and sent when they are back in touch.

**Cannot reach iPhone. As of 10:42.** What you see is from that time. Bring the phone closer, or open the app on it.

**It had already moved on.** The run moved on the iPhone before your press arrived, so nothing was done.

**A routine is missing.** A Focus on the iPhone may be hiding it. See [Place and Focus](place-and-focus.md).
