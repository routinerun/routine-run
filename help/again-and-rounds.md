# Again and rounds

**Again** does the step you are on once more: its timer starts over, and anything it does when it is reached happens again. **Rounds** are for a group of steps that repeats. The routine says which steps and how many times, and each run writes them out as steps of their own.

## Again on a step

Again sits between **Not now…** and **Next** on the step's card, and is also on the Lock Screen card and the watch.

It appears only where doing the step again changes something: the step has a count, a timer, or something it does when it is reached or when its time runs out.

To give a step a count, open it, then **Settings → How many times**, and set **Do it**. **Counts** decides what happens when the run leaves the step and comes back: **Each visit** starts again, **The whole run** carries on. Again goes away when the count is used up; **Next** never does.

To take Again off the Lock Screen card, turn off **Repeat** in the routine's **Settings → Live Activity**.

## Again on a finished run

A finished run's page has **Reopen**, **Again** and **Delete**. Again starts a new run from the first step, and the old one stays as it was. **Again later…** plans one for **In an hour**, **This evening**, **Tomorrow** or **Next week**.

## Rounds

1. Open the routine, then its row of steps.
2. Press the plus, then **Rounds**. It is offered once the routine has two steps.
3. Choose **From** and **To**. The same step in both is one step going round.
4. Choose **A set number** and how many, or **Until I stop**.
5. Press **Done**. The sheet shows how many steps a run will have.

When a run starts, it writes the rounds out as separate steps, named like "Stretch · round 2 of 4". Each round keeps its own notes and values. With **Until I stop**, the run writes one round. For another, open the run's list of steps, press the plus, then **Another round**.

Change a step on the routine, and every round made from it follows. Change one round inside a run, say 26 minutes instead of 25, and the band counts it. **Keep** writes it into the step the rounds come from. See [Keep or Only here](keep-or-only-here.md).

## When something goes wrong

- **There is no Again.** The step has nothing to do again, or its count is used up. Give it a count or a timer.
- **Rounds is missing from the plus.** The routine has rounds already. The **Rounds** row on its list of steps changes them.
- **A going run has no rounds.** They are written when a run starts.
