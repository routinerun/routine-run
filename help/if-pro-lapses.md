# If Pro lapses

Nothing is deleted and nothing stops working. Every routine you have keeps running; you just cannot add a new one past 3 until you have fewer, or Pro again.

## What keeps working

- **Every routine you already have**, all of them, however many.
- **Their triggers.** A routine that starts at seven still starts at seven. Nothing is ever locked at the moment it fires.
- **Runs that are going**, and the record of every run before.
- **Changing a routine.** Editing what you have is not adding.

## What waits

- **A new routine**, while you have 3 or more. Have fewer, or take Pro again, and the next one is yours to add.

The 3 are the ones you are keeping: archived routines do not count, so archiving one is how you have fewer. A routine with a run still going counts whichever shelf it is on — archiving it does not end the run, so the run is still yours and so is the routine. Finish or stop it and the count comes down.

## Why it works this way

A card asking you to pay, where your routine should have been at seven in the morning, is the worst moment to ask. So the limit sits where you add, never where a routine runs.
