# Archive, Delete and Recently Deleted

Getting rid of a routine takes three steps, and each can be undone until the last. **Archive** puts it out of the way. **Delete**, only on something archived, moves it to Recently Deleted for 30 days. **Delete Now**, or the 30 days passing, takes it for good.

## Archive

On the routine's page, press **Archive**. It does not ask, because nothing here is lost: the routine leaves the list and keeps everything — its runs, its history, its notes — and anything of it going right now carries on exactly where it was. Archive says you are not starting it again, not that you are stopping what is in hand.

To find it again, open the ellipsis in the Routines tab and, under **Show**, choose **Archived**. **Take out** on its page puts it back in the list.

## Delete

Delete is offered only for an archived routine. On its page, open the ellipsis and choose **Delete**, or hold its row in the list.

It asks only if runs of it are still going. A bar at the foot of the screen says it was deleted, with **Undo**. Everything the routine already ran stays in History.

A run is deleted the same way, once it has ended. In Runs, choose **Ended** the same way, then swipe the run or hold it and choose **Delete**. An ended run's page has **Delete** too. A run still going has to be stopped first.

## Recently Deleted

A **Recently Deleted** row appears when there is something in it: under the archived routines, under the ended runs, and in **Settings › Storage**.

Each row says how long it has left, such as "12 days left" or "Goes today". On each:

- **Restore** brings a routine back to the archive, with every run of it linked again. It brings a run back to History with its notes and values.
- **Delete Now** takes it for good. It asks first, because this cannot be undone.

![Recently Deleted with one routine in it, and the days it has left.](recently-deleted)

**More** at the top has **Restore All** and **Delete All Now**.

## What stays, whatever you do

- The lines a run wrote in the log.
- The runs of a deleted routine. Each keeps the routine as it was when the run ended, so its page still shows the steps, and **Again** can bring the routine back.

## With iCloud on

Deleting, restoring and Delete Now reach every device. A routine taken for good is not brought back by a device that was offline at the time. See [iCloud sync](icloud-sync.md).

## When it goes wrong

**There is no Delete on the routine.** It is not archived yet. Archive it first.

**There is no Recently Deleted row.** Nothing is in it.

**I deleted everything by mistake.** A backup from **Settings › Storage** puts back what is missing. So do the daily copies listed there under **Copies on this phone**.
